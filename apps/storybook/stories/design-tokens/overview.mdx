Design tokens are the visual atoms of the **@aazucena** design system. They ensure a consistent and maintainable visual language across the entire monorepo, translating abstract brand intent into mathematical code.

## The Token Hierarchy

Our tokens are organized into three distinct levels of abstraction to ensure maximum scalability and aesthetic flexibility.

### 1. Primitive Tokens
The bedrock values (colors, spacing, typography). These are raw values that represent the full capability of the system.
*   **[Colors](/docs/design-tokens-colors--docs)** — OKLCH scales.
*   **[Typography](/docs/design-tokens-typography--docs)** — Mathematical type scales.
*   **[Layout & Spacing](/docs/design-tokens-layout-spacing--docs)** — 4px baseline grid.

### 2. Functional Tokens
Tokens that represent an intended use or role. These refer to primitives but add semantic meaning.
*   **[Effects & Shadows](/docs/design-tokens-effects-shadows--docs)** — Volumetric depth.
*   **[Motion & Animation](/docs/design-tokens-motion-animation--docs)** — Physical interaction laws.
*   **[Layering & Z-Index](/docs/design-tokens-layering-z-index--docs)** — Stacking engine.

### 3. Vibe Tokens (The Theme System)
The highest level of abstraction. A **Vibe** is an orchestrated collection of token overrides that transforms the entire application's aesthetic.
*   **[Theme System](/docs/design-tokens-theme-system--docs)** — Visual Identity Protocol.
*   **[Explore Themes](/docs/design-tokens-themes-default--docs)** — Individual Vibe specs.
