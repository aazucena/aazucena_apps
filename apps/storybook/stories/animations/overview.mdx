# Animation System

A sophisticated, performance-tiered motion orchestration engine for the Aldrin Azucena portfolio.
Powered by GSAP, Three.js, and PixiJS for immersive atmospheric storytelling.

---

## Technical Stack

| System | Protocol | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **GSAP** | `The_Orchestrator` | Entrance/exit animations, scroll-driven transitions, and complex timeline sequencing. |
| **Three.js** | `The_Environment` | High-fidelity 3D background scenes, atmospheric layers, and depth-aware object interaction. |
| **PixiJS** | `The_Atmosphere` | High-performance 2D particle systems for dense environmental details (stars, snow, debris). |

---

## 🔍 Inspection Tooling: ObjectViewer

The `ObjectViewer` is a professional-grade 3D inspection component designed for auditing and showcasing assets within the `@aazucena/animations` registry.

### Key Capabilities
- **360° Inspection:** Fully interactive `OrbitControls` with zoom, pan, and auto-rotate modes.
- **Environment Logic:** Integrated HDR switcher to test materials under 5 lighting presets (City, Night, Warehouse, etc.).
- **Performance HUD:** Real-time visibility of **Cost Tiers (1-8)** and **Atmospheric Layer** assignments.
- **Diagnostic Helpers:** Toggles for coordinate grids, wireframes, bounding boxes, and XYZ origin axes.
- **Action Feedback:** High-fidelity animations for actions, including a camera shutter flash for screenshots and spinning resets.
- **System Notifications:** Integrated `sonner` toast system for action confirmation.

## 🦅 Mechanical Fidelity

We prioritize high-fidelity mechanical logic for our 3D assets to ensure they feel "alive" rather than static decorations.

- **IK Pedaling:** Character feet are mathematically locked to circular pedal paths using Law of Cosines solvers.
- **Shoulder Joint Pivots:** Wing flapping mechanics rotate from the shoulder joint for realistic avian motion.
- **Flight Profiles:** Objects support multiple behavioral variants (e.g., `majestic` vs `frantic`) via custom configuration.
- **Canon Precision:** Pop-culture assets follow strict color palettes and geometric landmarks (e.g., Planet Express overbite and racing stripes).
