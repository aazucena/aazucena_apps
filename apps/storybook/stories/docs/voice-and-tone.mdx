# Voice & Tone

The **@aazucena** voice is the bridge between human intuition and machine precision. It is helpful, competent, and technically authentic.

---

## The Core Voice: "The Helpful Engineer"

Our voice should sound like a highly competent peer—someone who respects your intelligence, values your time, and provides precise data to help you move forward.

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| **Helpful** | We don't just state facts; we provide actionable intelligence. |
| **Direct** | We get to the point. No "burying the lede" with apologies or fluff. |
| **Technical** | We use precise terminology (e.g., "0x4F2A" over "Address 1") when it adds trust. |
| **Empathetic** | We meet the user where they are (e.g., calm in errors, precise in analysis). |

---

## Writing Guidelines (Based on Tubik Studio Tips)

### 1. Design for Scannability
Users scan; they don't read.
*   **Do:** Use bold headers, bulleted lists, and keep primary messages at the top.
*   **Don't:** Write long "walls of text" in modals or status reports.

### 2. Conciseness Over Grammar
Prioritize directness and clarity over strict traditional grammar rules in microcopy.
*   **Do:** `Save changes?` (Active, direct).
*   **Don't:** `Would you like to save the changes you have made?` (Passive, wordy).

### 3. Precision Through Numbers
Use numerals to grab attention and convey exactness.
*   **Do:** `3 files updated` or `12ms latency`.
*   **Don't:** `Three files updated` or `Low latency`.

### 4. Jargon vs. Precision
Replace generic "marketing jargon" with simple everyday language, but retain "technical precision" where appropriate.
*   **Do:** `Allow microphone` (Simple action) or `Initialize Neural Link` (Brand technical).
*   **Don't:** `Utilize the audio input device` (Stilted jargon).

## Tone Map: Contextual Adaptation

While our **Voice** remains constant, our **Tone** adapts to the system's state.

| Context | Tone | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | Welcoming & Informative | "Welcome to the Engine. Initializing your technical profile." |
| **Normal State** | Professional & Focused | "Node Alpha is operational. Syncing 14 active data streams." |
| **Warning/Error** | Urgent & Precise | "Critical: Latency threshold reached (45ms). Rerouting packets." |
| **Success** | Concise & Affirmative | "Sync complete. 1,420 records indexed." |
