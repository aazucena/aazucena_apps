Charts take their colors from `chartTheme`, so they follow the vibe and light/dark mode. The D3 schemes (`schemeTableau10`, `interpolateBlues`) and 63 hard-coded colors are gone: series use the four locked series colors, then four tints mixed toward the panel in OKLab (`categorical`, `categoricalScale`). Heatmaps and choropleths bin values onto the five-step sequential ramp, and empty cells use the new `chartTheme.empty` role. Every chart shares one tooltip (`chartTooltip`): raised panel, 1px rule, mono text. `ChartContainer` renders as a Panel, and the chart controls are 30px Avra controls with `aria-pressed` and accessible names. `ChartExportControl` is a disclosure with Escape to close. `ChoroplethMap`'s `colors` prop is now optional; when it is omitted, values bin onto the ramp. Apps that don't load `css-vars.css` import `@aazucena/visualizations/chart.css`, generated by `ds:structure`. Dark series 3 (violet) is lighter and light series 2 (olive) is darker, so panel-colored labels on every series reach 4.5:1.
