Every vibe now brings its own accent: `--signal` and its tints, plus `--panel`, `--panel-raised`, `--rule`, `--rule-strong`, `--state` and `--series-1`, are derived from a vibe's legacy roles when it doesn't define them. Seasonal vibes no longer show the default cyan on buttons, badges and focus rings, and each vibe's signal, signal text and alert text pass AA on its own panel.
