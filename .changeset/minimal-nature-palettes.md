The Minimal and Nature vibes bring their own palette in both modes. Minimal dark sits on neutral zinc surfaces and Nature dark on green-tinted `forest` surfaces, instead of the default vibe's blue ink. Minimal light uses the Avra status colors (success was black, destructive was grey), and both light modes have a selection tone that differs from the panel. Nature light's success darkens to pass AA. New color steps: `zinc` 850 and the `forest` scale.
